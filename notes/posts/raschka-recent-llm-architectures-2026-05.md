# Recent Developments in LLM Architectures: KV Sharing, mHC, and Compressed Attention

> *Sebastian Raschka* — Ahead of AI, 2026-05-16

## TL;DR

Survey of architecture innovations in recent open-weight LLMs, focused on efficiency for long-context processing. Covers: cross-layer key-value sharing (Gemma 4), per-layer embeddings, layer-wise attention budgeting (Laguna XS.2), compressed convolutional attention (ZAYA1-8B), and manifold-constrained hyper-connections plus compressed attention in DeepSeek V4.
