# General Agent: A Self-Evolving, Synthetic Agent Environment

> *Mika (Prime Intellect)* — Prime Intellect blog, 2026-05-18

## TL;DR

Prime Intellect open-sources `general-agent`, a fully synthetic agent environment that grows its own task corpus over time. Uses a synthesizer/solver loop: a synthesizer generates progressively harder tasks across five difficulty tiers, a solver validates them. The released corpus has 4,504 tasks spanning 1,040 domains with 8,000+ unique tools.
