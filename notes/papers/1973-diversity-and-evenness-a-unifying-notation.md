# Diversity and Evenness: A Unifying Notation and Its Consequences (Hill 1973)

## Citation

- DOI: 10.2307/1934352 — *Ecology* 54(2), pp. 427–432, 1973.
- Author: M. O. Hill.
- Pre-arXiv-era; access via journal / JSTOR.

## Core claim (stub)

Defines **Hill numbers** $^qD = (\sum p_i^q)^{1/(1-q)}$, a parameterised family that unifies Shannon, Simpson, Berger–Parker, and other diversity indices on a single scale (effective number of equally common types). The continuous extension at $q = 1$ is $\exp(H)$. This is the foundational notation underpinning [[metrics/hill-numbers]] and the multiplicative α/β/γ decomposition in [[notes/papers/2007-partitioning-diversity-into-independent-alpha-and-beta-components]].

## Why it's load-bearing

The α/β/γ decomposition the KB uses (`concepts/diversity.md` §Bridging local to global) is built on Hill numbers. Without this stub the formalism's roots are unrecorded.
