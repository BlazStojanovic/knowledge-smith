# RLVE: Scaling Up Reinforcement Learning for Language Models with Adaptive Verifiable Environments

> *Zhiyuan Zeng, Hamish Ivison, Yiping Wang…* — arXiv 2511.07317, 2025

## TL;DR

(stub — fill in after reading)

## Abstract

This work presents RLVE, a method employing verifiable environments that dynamically adjust problem difficulty in relation to model advancement during training. The approach creates a comprehensive suite of 400 verifiable environments called RLVE-Gym through systematic engineering. Experimental results demonstrate that expanding the quantity of training environments yields consistent enhancements in reasoning generalization. Joint training utilizing all 400 environments achieves a 3.37% absolute average improvement across six reasoning benchmarks for a 1.5B parameter model, substantially outperforming continued standard reinforcement learning training which yielded only 0.49% improvement despite requiring more than triple the computational resources. The authors have made their implementation publicly available.
