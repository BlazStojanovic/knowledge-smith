# R2E-Gym

- **arXiv**: [2504.07164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07164)
- **Authors / affiliation**: Naman Jain, Jaskirat Singh, Manish Shetty, Liang Zheng, Koushik Sen, Ion Stoica (UC Berkeley)
- **Year / venue**: 2025, COLM 2025
- **Raw**: [[raw/papers/pdf/2025-r2e-gym]]
- **Grounding axis**: [[maps/grounding/repository-multifile]] (procedurally generated environments)
- **Output shape**: Agent trajectories over executable repos + hybrid-verifier re-ranking signals
- **Filter / verification**: Hybrid — execution-based verifier + execution-free (LM) verifier, combined at inference time.
- **Training stage**: Trajectory-SFT + verifier training

## Method

Two contributions:

1. **SWE-GEN** — synthetic data curation recipe that produces executable gym environments *without* requiring human-written pull requests or unit tests. This decouples the supply of training environments from the supply of real PRs.
2. **Hybrid inference-time verifier** — combines an execution-based verifier and an execution-free (LM) verifier; the paper reports better performance than either alone.

Together: 8.1K problems across 13 repos, each with an executable gym environment, unit tests, and natural-language task description.

## Key result

Reported **51%** on SWE-Bench Verified — new state-of-the-art for open-weight SWE agents at release, reported as first time being competitive with proprietary models like o1 and Claude 3.5 Sonnet v2 with tools.

## Notes

- Contrast with [[notes/papers/2024-swe-gym]]: SWE-Gym uses real repos with real PRs; R2E-Gym generates environments procedurally. Both report real agent rollouts (filtered by task success) as the training data — the difference is the origin of the *environment*, not the trajectory.
- Also discussed in [[concepts/trajectory-synthesis]] as the canonical procedurally-generated-environment example.
