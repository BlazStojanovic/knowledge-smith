# Cosmopedia: How to Create Large-Scale Synthetic Data for Pre-training Large Language Models

Large-scale synthetic textbook and educational content generation for LLM pretraining.

- **Authors**: Loubna Ben Allal, Anton Lozhkov, Daniel van Strien, et al. (HuggingFace)
- **Year**: 2024
- **URL**: [huggingface.co/blog/cosmopedia](https://huggingface.co/blog/cosmopedia)
- **Type**: HuggingFace blog post (no arXiv paper)

## Core contribution

Describes the creation of Cosmopedia, a large-scale synthetic dataset of textbooks, blog posts, stories, and educational content generated by Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct. Covers the methodology for generating diverse, high-quality synthetic pretraining data at scale: topic seeding from web data, prompt curation, and quality filtering. Used to train the SmolLM family of models.
