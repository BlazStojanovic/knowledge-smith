# OSCAR: Open Super-large Crawled Aggregated coRpus

## Citation

- Authors: Pedro Javier Ortiz Suárez, Benoît Sagot, Laurent Romary (Inria)
- Year / venue: 2019, CMLC-7 Workshop (Challenges in the Management of Large Corpora), Cardiff
- Note: No arXiv preprint. Workshop publication.

## Short Summary

Pipeline for building large multilingual monolingual corpora from CommonCrawl. Reproduced and optimized the CommonCrawl extraction pipeline with language identification. First version: 20+ TB of plain text across many languages. Canonical early reference for rule-based web-scale pretraining data extraction. OSCAR v2 (2022, arXiv [2201.06642](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.06642)) later introduced document-level processing and improved cleaning.
