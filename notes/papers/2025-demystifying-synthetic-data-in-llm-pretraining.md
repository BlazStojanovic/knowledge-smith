# Demystifying Synthetic Data in LLM Pre-training: A Systematic Study of Scaling Laws, Benefits, and Pitfalls

Systematic study of scaling laws for synthetic data in pretraining, identifying benefits and failure modes.

- **Authors**: Feiyang Kang, Newsha Ardalani, Michael Kuchnik, Youssef Emad, Mostafa Elhoushi, Shubhabrata Sengupta, Shang-Wen Li, Ramya Raghavendra, Ruoxi Jia, Carole-Jean Wu
- **Venue**: EMNLP 2025
- **arXiv**: [2510.01631](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01631)
- **Raw**: [[raw/papers/pdf/2025-demystifying-synthetic-data]]

## Core contribution

Systematic empirical study of how synthetic data affects LLM pretraining at scale. Characterizes scaling laws specific to synthetic data, identifies when synthetic data helps vs. hurts, and documents pitfalls including mode collapse and quality degradation at scale.
