# PARTNR: A Benchmark for Planning and Reasoning in Embodied Multi-Agent Tasks

## Citation

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00081
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.00081
- Authors: Matthew Chang, Gunjan Chhablani, Alexander Clegg, et al.
- Year / venue: 2024
- arXiv: 2411.00081

## Core Claim

Multi-agent embodied benchmark where LLMs help design and decompose collaboration tasks, ground them in simulators (Habitat), and export standard task collections with planner baselines. Defines evaluation via propositions with temporal constraints; percent completion as satisfied-proposition ratio.
