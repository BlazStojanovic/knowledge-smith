# RARR: Researching and Revising What Language Models Say, Using Language Models

Post-hoc revision of LLM outputs by comparing against retrieved evidence.

- **Authors**: Luyu Gao, Zhuyun Dai, Panupong Pasupat, Tsz-Kin Lam, Mia Zhao, Ming-Wei Chang, et al.
- **Venue**: NAACL 2023
- **arXiv**: [2210.08726](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.08726)
- **Raw**: [[raw/papers/pdf/2023-rarr]]

## Core contribution

RARR (Retrofit Attribution using Research and Revision): takes a model's draft output, retrieves evidence from external sources, and revises the draft to be consistent with retrieved facts while preserving fluency. Acts as a post-hoc attribution and correction layer, ensuring only verified content survives.
