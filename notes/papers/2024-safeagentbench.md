# SafeAgentBench: A Benchmark for Safe Task Planning of Embodied LLM Agents

## Citation

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13178
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.13178
- Authors: Sheng Yin, Xianghe Pang, Yuanzhuo Ding, et al.
- Year / venue: 2024
- arXiv: 2412.13178

## Core Claim

Benchmark for evaluating safety of embodied LLM agents via hazardous task rejection. Reports low rejection rates and non-trivial risk for current embodied LLMs, motivating explicit tracking of hazard rejection and risk rates.
