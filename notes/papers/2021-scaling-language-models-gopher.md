# Scaling Language Models: Methods, Analysis & Insights from Training Gopher

## Citation

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.11446
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.11446
- Authors: Jack W. Rae et al. (DeepMind, 57 co-authors)
- Year / venue: 2021-12 arXiv preprint

## Short Summary

280B parameter language model (Gopher) evaluated on 152 tasks. Introduces the MassiveText dataset and its curation pipeline including heuristic quality filtering rules (the "Gopher rules"). Relevant to data filtering: identifies that perplexity-based filtering biases toward Wikipedia-like content, which is valuable text but penalizes valuable unconventional content (code, conversational text, technical writing). Gains from scale are largest in reading comprehension and fact-checking; logical and mathematical reasoning benefit less.
