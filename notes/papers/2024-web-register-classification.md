# Untangling the Unrestricted Web: Automatic Identification of Multilingual Registers

## Citation

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19892
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.19892
- Authors: Erik Henriksson, Amanda Myntti, et al.
- Year / venue: 2024-06 arXiv preprint

## Short Summary

Deep learning models for automatic identification of registers (text varieties such as news reports, discussion forums, instructional text) in web-based datasets across 16 languages. Uses the Multilingual CORE corpora with 25 registers designed to cover the full unrestricted web. Multi-label approach achieves near-human-level performance. Provides the register classifier used in subsequent work by Myntti et al. 2025 and HPLT 3.0 for register-based data curation.
