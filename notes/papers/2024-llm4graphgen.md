# Exploring the Potential of Large Language Models in Graph Generation

## Citation

- arXiv: 2403.14358
- Authors: Yao et al.
- Year: 2024

## Core Claim

Demonstrates that LLMs (GPT-4 etc.) can generate graph structures directly from natural language prompts, covering rule-based and distribution-based tasks. Shows limitations in structural fidelity for complex distributional parameters (motif counts). Defines validity, novelty, and uniqueness metrics for graph generation evaluation.

## Relevance

Cited by [[notes/papers/2026-the-llm-data-auditor-a-metric-oriented-survey-on-quality-and-trustworthiness-in-evaluating-synthetic-data]] §5.1.1, §5.2.1 as the primary reference for training-free graph generation and the source of Valid_rule, Novelty, and Uniqueness metrics.
