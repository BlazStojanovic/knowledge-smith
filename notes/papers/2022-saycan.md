# Do As I Can, Not As I Say: Grounding Language in Robotic Affordances

## Citation

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.01691
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.01691
- Authors: Michael Ahn, Anthony Brohan, Noah Brown, et al.
- Year / venue: 2022
- arXiv: 2204.01691

## Core Claim

Combines LLM language understanding with value estimates from robot interaction data to ground language commands in feasible robotic actions, enabling language-conditioned planning that respects physical affordances.
