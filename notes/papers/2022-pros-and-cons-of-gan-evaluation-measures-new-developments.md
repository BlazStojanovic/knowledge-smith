# Pros and Cons of GAN Evaluation Measures: New Developments (Borji 2022)

## Citation

- arXiv: [2206.10927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10927)
- Author: Ali Borji.
- Year / venue: arXiv 2022 (extends Borji 2018 survey).

## Core claim (stub)

A landscape survey of generative-model evaluation metrics: FID, Inception Score, Precision/Recall variants, density/coverage, MAUVE-style approaches, perceptual metrics (LPIPS), and many others. The "new developments" framing covers the post-2018 wave. Useful as an entry-point catalogue when chasing a novel metric back to its origins.

## Why it's load-bearing

Survey paper. Worth a stub for the citation graph; not load-bearing on its own but useful when a deep-dive on a specific metric needs context.

## Caveats

- Vision-domain biased. Most metrics covered are FID-family for images; text-domain analogues (MAUVE, semantic-embedding precision/recall) are the relevant transfer.
