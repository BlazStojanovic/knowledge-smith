# WizardLM: Empowering Large Language Models to Follow Complex Instructions

Evolutionary instruction mutation (Evol-Instruct) to systematically increase instruction complexity.

- **Authors**: Can Xu, Qingfeng Sun, Kai Zheng, Xiubo Geng, Pu Zhao, Jiazhan Feng, Chongyang Tao, Daxin Jiang
- **Venue**: ICLR 2024
- **arXiv**: [2304.12244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.12244)
- **Raw**: [[raw/papers/pdf/2024-evol-instruct-wizardlm]]

## Core contribution

Introduces Evol-Instruct: mutation operators that systematically rewrite instructions to increase constraints, reasoning depth, and complexity. Generates complex instruction data from simple seed instructions through iterative evolution. WizardLM-70B approaches GPT-4 on several benchmarks.
