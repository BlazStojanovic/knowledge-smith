# LIMA: Less Is More for Alignment

## Citation

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11206
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.11206
- Authors: Chunting Zhou, Pengfei Liu, Puxin Xu, Srinivasan Iyer, Jiao Sun, Yuning Mao, Xuezhe Ma, Avia Efrat, Ping Yu, Lili Yu, Susan Zhang, Gargi Ghosh, Mike Lewis, Luke Zettlemoyer, Omer Levy
- Year / venue: NeurIPS 2023
- arXiv: 2305.11206

## Short Summary

Demonstrates that 1,000 carefully curated examples are sufficient to fine-tune a strong pretrained LLM (LLaMA 65B) to produce high-quality responses, achieving competitive performance with GPT-4-trained models. Core claim: almost all knowledge in LLMs is learned during pretraining; alignment is primarily about learning the style/format of interaction, not new knowledge.
