# Dolma: An Open Corpus of Three Trillion Tokens for Language Model Pretraining Research

Open, reproducible pretraining corpus with transparent mixture design and documented curation pipeline.

- **Authors**: Luca Soldaini, Rodney Kinney, Akshita Bhagia, Dustin Schwenk, David Wadden, Noah A. Smith, Jesse Dodge, Kyle Lo, et al.
- **Venue**: ACL 2024
- **arXiv**: [2402.00159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00159)
- **Raw**: [[raw/papers/pdf/2024-dolma]]

## Core contribution

Three-trillion-token open pretraining corpus with fully documented and reproducible data curation pipeline. Enables practitioners to adjust domain coverage and data budgets without conflating quantity with quality. Released with reproducible tooling.
