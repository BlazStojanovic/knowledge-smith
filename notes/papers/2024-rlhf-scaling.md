# Does RLHF Scale? Exploring the Impacts from Data, Model, and Method

## Citation

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06000
- Year / venue: 2024 / arXiv
- arXiv: 2412.06000

## Core Claim

RLHF does not scale as effectively as pretraining. Performance improves early but additional data yields marginal gains, unlike pretraining's consistent power-law scaling.
