# Eureka: Human-Level Reward Design via Coding Large Language Models

## Core Claim

LLMs write and iteratively refine reward functions as executable code, validated through downstream policy optimisation in simulation environments. Achieves human-level reward design on diverse manipulation and locomotion tasks.
