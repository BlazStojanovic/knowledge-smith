# GLM-5.1

Zhipu's flagship MoE, scaled to ~744B total parameters. Adopts DeepSeek-style **MLA + DeepSeek Sparse Attention** at flagship scale — a clear architectural pivot away from GLM-4.5's GQA + attention-bias recipe toward DeepSeek V3.2's attention stack. Per Raschka, GLM-5.1 is a post-training refresh on top of the GLM-5 backbone; architecture is shared.

## Key Architecture Choices

- **Adoption of DeepSeek's attention stack**: combines [[notes/model-cards/2024-deepseek-v3|MLA]] with [[notes/model-cards/2025-deepseek-v3-2|DeepSeek Sparse Attention]] — a clear pivot away from GLM-4.5's GQA + attention-bias recipe. Reflects continued convergence on DeepSeek V3.2 as the de-facto frontier MoE blueprint.
- **MTP-capable backbone**: supports multi-token prediction, enabling speculative decoding at inference.
- **GLM-5.1 = post-training refresh of GLM-5**: per Raschka, architecture stays aligned with GLM-5; the differentiating change is post-training, not architectural.
- **Scale jump from GLM-4.5**: 355B → 744B total (~2× total) and 33B → 40B active. Activation ratio drops from ~9% (GLM-4.5) to ~5.4% (GLM-5.1) — sparser activation in line with DeepSeek-style MoE recipes.
