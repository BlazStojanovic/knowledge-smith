# OLMo 2

Allen AI's second-generation fully open language model family. Notable for adopting post-norm RMSNorm placement (after attention/FFN) and QK-norm for improved training stability.

## Key Architecture Choices

- **Post-norm placement**: normalization applied after attention and FFN sublayers rather than before (pre-norm). Improves training stability and avoids the need for warmup-heavy schedules. Distinct from most contemporary LLMs which use pre-norm.
- **QK-norm**: applies RMSNorm to query and key projections before the dot-product attention computation, preventing attention logit growth at scale.
- **MHA → GQA transition**: 7B uses full multi-head attention (32/32); 32B switches to grouped-query attention (40 heads, 8 KV groups) for inference efficiency at scale.
- **SwiGLU-style FFN**: uses SiLU activation with gated linear unit structure. FFN ratio ~2.7× for 7B, ~5.4× for 32B.
