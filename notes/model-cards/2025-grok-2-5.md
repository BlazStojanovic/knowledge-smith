# Grok 2.5

xAI's production MoE model. Uses a classic MoE approach with 8 large experts, representing the older trend of fewer, larger experts compared to the fine-grained routing used by DeepSeek V3 or Qwen3.

## Key Architecture Choices

- **Classic MoE (8 large experts)**: represents the older paradigm of fewer, larger experts. Contrasts with the trend toward many fine-grained experts (DeepSeek V3: 256, Qwen3: 128, Qwen3-Next: 1024).
- **Shared expert with doubled intermediate**: the shared expert uses 2× the intermediate dimension of routed experts, giving it disproportionate capacity for capturing common patterns.
- **Production system**: described by Raschka as a real production flagship, providing a reference point for how MoE designs perform at scale in real deployment.
