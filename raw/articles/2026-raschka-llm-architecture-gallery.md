Full content at source URL. This raw entry serves as an attribution pointer for model-architecture notes sourced from this gallery — a companion / live update to [[raw/articles/2026-raschka-big-llm-architecture-comparison]].

Coverage extends the comparison article with newer 2026 releases including: Gemma 4, GLM-5 / GLM-5.1, Qwen3.6 (dense and MoE), Kimi K2.6, DeepSeek V4 (Flash and Pro). Each card lists per-model layer counts, attention mechanism, expert configuration (for MoE), context length, vocab size, and KV-cache size class.

Gallery cards are terse — they list architectural facts without defining novel terms (e.g., "MLA-style CSA/HCA with mHC", "p-RoPE", "hash-based routing"). For depth on novel mechanisms, follow the source paper / technical report from the model's developer rather than relying on the gallery alone.
